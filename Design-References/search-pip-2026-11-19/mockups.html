FirePlayer 2.0 · mock-ups · 19 Nov 2026

Search and the live picture

Twelve screens to choose from before anything is built. Each is the app as it runs today with the new parts drawn in, at the TV’s own size. Tap a screen to see it larger. Two questions come with two options each, and my pick is marked.

ASearch for Movies & TV Shows

What the rail’s Search opens. The bar and the history pods now have the glass of the guide’s programme pods, and their softer corners.

Recommended: option 2. It reads the same with or without the live picture, and Down from the bar still goes straight to the results.

  • The rail’s Search opens here with focus on the bar. OK opens the keyboard.
  • Up from the bar reaches the two kinds (Right, in option 1). OK switches kind; each keeps its own history.
  • Down goes to the history: OK searches it again, holding OK offers Remove. The bin clears the history after asking.
  • Back goes to the rail.

BSearch for Live TV

Opened from the Search bubble in the Live TV settings window, or with the Live TV pill. It looks through the channels and programmes of every playlist.

CThe live picture on Home

Leave a playing channel for Home and it keeps playing top right, where Recent channels is today.

Recommended: option 1. The picture is why the box is there, and one picture over one quiet strip reads best from the sofa.

  • The channel keeps playing, with its sound, when you go from Live TV to Home. Focus stays on the page.
  • Starting a film or an episode stops it. Going back to Live TV carries on with the same channel.
  • Channel up and down on the remote still change channel here, when Remote is set to Channel buttons only.
  • Right from Details, or holding Back, moves to the picture’s buttons. Left and Right move between Volume, Full screen and Close (Up and Down in option 2); Down goes to the rows.
  • Open questionFocus lands on Full screen, so OK straight away opens the channel full screen on Live TV; the picture itself is not a stop. Or should it land on Volume, the nearest button?
  • Open questionVolume: OK turns the picture’s sound off and on. Or should it open a volume level?
  • Open questionBack from the buttons returns to where you were on the page. Right?

DThe live picture on Search

The same picture and strip, a size smaller (a quarter of the screen wide), in the top corner beside the page’s name. The bar ends before it and the results start under it.

What I need from you

  1. The switch between the two kinds of search (A1): option 1, at the end of the bar, or option 2, two pills above the bar. I recommend 2.
  2. The live picture on Home (C1, C2): option 1, the picture fills the box, or option 2, a smaller picture over Recent channels. I recommend 1.
  3. First button (C2): focus lands on Full screen, or on Volume?
  4. Volume (C2): sound off and on, or a volume level?
  5. Back from the buttons (C2): returns to where you were on the page?
  6. After Close (C3): focus on the first recent channel, or back where you were on the page?
  7. The other tabs (D1): does the picture stop on Movies, TV Shows, My List and Settings, or follow you there?
  8. Wording on Live TV search (B1): “Search channels and programmes”, “Channels”, “Programmes”. Keep or change?

The still inside the live picture and the programme titles in B2 and B3 are stand-ins. Channel names, logos, posters and everything around them are cut from the app as it runs on the TV today.
